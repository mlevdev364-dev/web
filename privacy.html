Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data SAKA OMNI collects, how it's used, and the choices you have.

The Service is not directed to individuals under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, with additional notice for material changes.

12. Contact us

For questions about this policy, contact us at hello@saka-ai.my.id.
